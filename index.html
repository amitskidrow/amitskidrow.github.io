How it works

Your account, your operations.

01

Authorize with Google

Choose your account in Google's browser sign-in flow and review the requested permissions. The terminal tools use rclone to obtain and renew access.

02

Run your transfers

The tools run on machines controlled by the operator. They support selected uploads, retrieval and encrypted recovery workflows. This website provides information about those tools.

03

Keep control of access

Credentials and encryption keys stay in the operator's private configuration. Access can be revoked through your Google Account. Existing files remain until you remove them.

Permission and encryption

Understand the access you grant.

The tools can request full Google Drive access so that they can work with existing files and backup locations. The media transfer workflow uses rclone crypt; recovery workflows encrypt backup data. Encryption does not conceal all account, size or timing metadata from the storage provider.
